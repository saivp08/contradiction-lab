# Two-minute demonstration

Before presenting, start the app and confirm the engine: with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set, runs use live Claude agents; otherwise say "deterministic specialists". A live debate on long papers takes about 15 minutes, so run one beforehand and present it as a **verified replay**, or use the bundled sample debate.

## 0:00–0:15 — The problem

"Two papers study the same question and reach different answers. Contradiction Lab reads both, finds exactly where they disagree, and turns that disagreement into the next experiment."

## 0:15–0:40 — Upload and evidence

Click **Compare two papers**, upload both PDFs and click **Analyze papers**. Point to the parsed metadata, the side-by-side quotes and **View evidence**: "Every quote is an exact sentence with its page number. Anything the parser can't find stays unknown." Point to the relationship banner and **What differs?**

If the papers don't disagree, the app says so and offers no investigation: "It refuses to manufacture a contradiction."

## 0:40–1:05 — The debate

Click **Start the investigation**. Narrate the Arena as the specialists pass evidence, the contradiction and hypotheses between seats, then pause at your seat. "Nothing runs without my approval." Approve the experiment.

## 1:05–1:35 — The critic

"The lab attacks its own result." Each challenge is rebutted, stands or stays open on a computed number. Point to the open challenge: "No shared primary dataset was analysed. Text alone can't settle who's right, and the lab says so."

## 1:35–2:00 — The decision

Open **Next move**: the decision, the evidence chain behind it (*Why this decision?*) and the measured activity panel. "No invented speed-up: these are counts and timings from this run." Close with **Replay verified run**: "The whole debate is sealed and replays exactly."
